# Candidate Positioning Archetypes

Positioning archetypes define the candidate lens used to frame achievements against target job descriptions.

## Recommended Archetypes

### 1. The Operator / Execution Lead
- **Focus**: Process optimization, cross-functional delivery, operational rigor, measurable KPIs.
- **Narrative**: High-agency builder who takes ambiguous problems and turns them into systematic, repeatable operational systems.
- **Key Proof Domains**: Efficiency gains, time-to-delivery compression, cross-functional stakeholder alignment.

### 2. The Strategist / Business Driver
- **Focus**: Market analysis, commercial growth, go-to-market strategy, revenue optimization.
- **Narrative**: Analytical problem-solver who bridges high-level vision with concrete commercial execution.
- **Key Proof Domains**: Revenue influence, market sizing, strategic planning, resource allocation.

### 3. The Builder / Entrepreneur
- **Focus**: Zero-to-one product development, full-stack ownership, customer acquisition, lean testing.
- **Narrative**: Scrappy founder who has owned every function from licensing and vendor sourcing to growth.
- **Key Proof Domains**: End-to-end venture creation, customer traction, cost optimization.
