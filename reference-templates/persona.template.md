# Candidate Persona & Voice

## The Core Standard
Direct, authoritative, concise, and grounded in real-world commercial outcomes.
Never sound like an enthusiastic AI generator or a generic corporate resume.

## Writing Guardrails
1. **Plain Human Authority**: Speak plainly. Use active, past-tense strong verbs.
2. **Work + Context + Outcome**: Every statement details what was built, in what context, producing what outcome.
3. **No Fluff or Buzzwords**: Eliminate words like "spearheaded", "passionate", "delve", "crucial", "testament", "vibrant".
4. **Zero Hyphens**: Never use hyphens (-) or em-dashes (—) in dynamic bullet text. Use commas, semicolons, or colons.
