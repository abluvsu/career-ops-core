# Candidate Proof Bank

The Proof Bank is the single source of truth for all quantitative claims and achievements.
Rules:
1. Never invent metrics.
2. Every number appearing in the CV must have a verifiable entry in this proof bank.
3. Keep plain-text character budgets within 95–115 characters (1 line) or 180–230 characters (2 full lines).
4. No hyphens or em-dashes in bullet points (Rule 2).
